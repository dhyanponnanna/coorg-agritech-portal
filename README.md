Coorg Agri-Tech Portal

PortalLocalized Hyper-Local Weather: A web dashboard providing precise rainfall and humidity predictions tailored to specific Kodagu taluks like Madikeri, Virajpet, and Somwarpet.

Crop Disease Diagnostic Tool: A simple portal where planters upload photos of coffee leaves or pepper vines to identify fungal diseases or pest infestations.

Labor Matchmaking Board: A specialized job board connecting estate owners with seasonal plantation workers during critical harvesting seasons.
